# Peer-to-Peer Fundraising Resources for Nonprofits

Peer-to-peer fundraising allows supporters to raise donations through their own networks. It can be useful for nonprofits, churches, schools, charities, and community organizations.

## What this resource covers

* Peer-to-peer fundraising
* Peer-to-peer fundraising software
* Team fundraising platforms
* Peer-to-peer donation platforms
* Peer-to-peer fundraising examples
* Tips for organizing supporter-led campaigns

## How Peer-to-Peer Fundraising Works

A nonprofit creates a campaign and invites supporters to participate. Individual supporters can then share the campaign with their friends, family, coworkers, and social networks.

A typical campaign includes:

1. Create a fundraising campaign
2. Recruit supporters
3. Create individual or team fundraising pages
4. Share the campaign
5. Collect donations
6. Track fundraising progress

## Peer-to-Peer Fundraising Examples

Some common examples include charity walks, birthday fundraisers, school campaigns, church fundraising, community challenges, and team-based fundraising events.
